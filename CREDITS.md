## Deliberately not used

While sourcing photos, a few candidates were rejected as inappropriate for this
demo even though their licenses would have technically allowed reuse:

- **A documentary-style photo of a specific, identifiable real house** (a Chicago
  bungalow street photo) — using a real family's actual home as a fake "for sale"
  listing risks real-world confusion about that property. This is different from the
  house/kitchen photos used above, which are deliberately generic, stock-style shots
  captioned for illustrative reuse.
- **A bakery/interior photo with legible foreign-language signage and price tags** —
  too obviously a specific, unrelated real business.
- **No agent headshot is used anywhere on this site.** The agent identity here is a
  placeholder (see README.md) — attaching a real stranger's photograph to a fabricated
  name, bio, and license number would misrepresent that person. Replace the `photo`
  field in `src/_data/business.json` with the real agent's own photo once this is a
  real engagement.
